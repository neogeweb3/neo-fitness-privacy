# Neo Fitness 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 1 日

Neo Fitness（以下简称「本应用」）是一款个人开发、处于测试阶段的健身与健康记录应用。本应用的个人信息处理者为本应用开发者（即本应用 App Store 页面列明的卖家，以下简称「开发者」）。本政策说明本应用收集哪些数据、如何使用、发送给谁、保存多久，以及您如何撤回同意和删除数据。

## 一、保存在您设备上的数据

本应用从「健康」App 读取的数据，以及您在本应用中记录的训练、饮食、围度、体重、血压、化验单与检查报告等，默认只保存在您的设备上，用于生成健康与训练趋势。

经您授权，本应用会将您录入的体重、腰围、血压、营养摄入、体成分（体脂率、去脂体重）及训练记录（含训练期间的心率、能量消耗和训练耗力评分）写入「健康」App。

## 二、启用 Neo Intelligence 后发送的数据

Neo Intelligence 包括新用户引导生成训练计划、对话问答、训练完成后的自动分析、饮食与围度的自动分析、训练周报与月报，以及每日将对话整理为记忆。只有在您同意后才会启用；未启用时，本应用不发送 AI 处理请求。您登录、管理或删除账号时，本应用仍需与 Apple 和 Neo Fitness 服务器通信。

启用后，为生成回答，本应用会将以下数据直接发送至大语言模型服务合作方处理。合作方的服务器位于中国内地，您的数据将传输至中国内地处理：

- 身体基本信息：年龄、性别、身高、体重、体成分；
- 心率、静息心率、心率变异性、最大摄氧量；
- 睡眠记录；
- 训练与活动记录、身体围度；
- 饮食与营养摄入、餐单与补剂设置；
- 血压记录（如您记录过）；
- 您选择让 AI 读取的化验单与检查报告文件；
- 您在对话中附加的照片、视频、PDF、文本和音频文件；
- 您在引导问题、对话中填写的内容，以及由对话整理出的记忆。

使用语音输入时，录音会发送至大语言模型服务合作方转写为文字。

本应用不申请读取生殖健康类数据（如月经、排卵、妊娠相关记录）。

## 三、数据的接收方

| 接收方 | 用途 |
|---|---|
| Neo Fitness 服务器（位于中国内地北京） | 验证 Apple 登录、保存必要的账号资料和 AI 使用量、按个人额度控制 AI 使用量、发放临时 AI 访问凭证及处理删除请求 |
| Apple | 登录认证及撤销授权 |
| 大语言模型服务合作方（位于中国内地） | 根据提交的数据生成回答；语音转写 |
| 美国国家医学图书馆（NLM）运营的 PubMed 服务（位于美国） | 文献检索：英文检索词、设备 IP 地址 |

除 PubMed 外，上述接收方均位于中国内地，相关数据将传输至中国内地处理。

大语言模型服务合作方仅在您启用 Neo Intelligence 后接收数据，且仅将其用于为您生成回答和转写语音。合作方受合同约束，对您的数据提供与本政策一致的保护；其数据处理条款禁止将您的数据用于训练或改进模型。合作方可能依其条款和适用法律，在有限期限内保存调用时产生的数据；开发者无法删除合作方保存的数据。

当您向 Neo Intelligence 提出涉及文献的问题时，本应用会将英文检索词发送至美国国家医学图书馆（NLM）运营的 PubMed 服务，检索词和您的设备 IP 地址将传输至美国。

开发者不出售您的数据，不将其用于广告，也不将其用于训练任何模型。

## 四、保存期限

- Neo Fitness 服务器保存账号标识、显示名、开发者为识别账号添加的备注、个人额度、注册及最近使用时间、App 版本、每周 AI 使用量统计和用于防止同一请求被重复计入额度的请求编号，用于登录和额度管理。开发者可查看账号与用量清单。账号资料和相关管理记录保留至删除账号；每周 AI 使用量统计和请求编号保留 35 天后到期自动删除，删除账号时立即删除；临时登录记录有效期为 5 分钟，过期后清理；Apple 授权令牌不长期保存。
- 赞或踩反馈仅保存在您的设备上，不上传至 Neo Fitness 服务器。对话、健康数据和文件均不保存在 Neo Fitness 服务器上。
- 服务器运行日志不包含问题、回答、健康数据、IP 地址或登录令牌。

## 五、您的权利、撤回同意与删除数据

- **撤回同意**：在「设置」的「Neo Intelligence」一栏点按「个性、自定义与隐私」（也可从对话页侧边菜单的「设置」进入），关闭「启用」开关。关闭后本应用立即停止新的 AI 数据发送；关闭时已经发出的请求会照常完成。您仍可登录、管理或删除账号。
- **清除 Neo Intelligence 内容**：在同一页面的「删除数据」一栏点按「清除 Neo Intelligence 内容」，无需先关闭 Neo Intelligence。此操作删除本机全部对话、附件和对话中待记录的饮食草稿，以及赞或踩反馈、记忆、「我的要求」、「改动记录」和本机缓存的个人概况；账号、个人额度、每周 AI 使用量统计和请求编号保留。本机训练、饮食、化验单与检查报告、体成分记录和新用户引导记录不受影响。大语言模型服务合作方保存的数据不在删除范围内。此操作无法撤销。
- **删除账号**：在同一页面的「账号」一栏点按您的账号，再点按「删除账号」并确认。服务器将立即删除账号资料、个人额度、每周 AI 使用量统计、请求编号和管理记录，同时清除本机 Neo Intelligence 内容，并撤销 Apple 授权；删除过程中使用的临时记录在清理完成后移除。删除请求未能完成时可重试。如无法完成 Apple 授权，可点按「无法完成 Apple 授权，继续删除」并确认；账号删除后，本应用将提示您在 Apple 账号设置中手动撤销 Neo Fitness 的授权，方法为：在 iPhone 上打开「设置」，点按您的姓名，点按「通过 Apple 登录」，选择 Neo Fitness，然后点按「删除」并按屏幕提示确认。账号删除后无法恢复；本机训练、饮食、化验单与检查报告、体成分记录和新用户引导记录不受影响。
- **退出与设备数据**：退出登录只清除本机登录凭证、临时 AI 访问凭证和用量缓存，保留本机记录；切换 Apple 账号前须确认清除上一账号的本机 Neo Intelligence 内容。账号不提供健康记录云同步；退出或删除账号不能远程擦除其他离线设备上的数据。已发出的临时 AI 访问凭证最多在 300 秒内仍然有效。删除本应用可清除本机其余健康、体成分及引导记录。已写入「健康」App 的数据需在「健康」App 中管理或删除；删除本应用不会删除这些数据。
- **查阅、更正与获取副本**：您可通过第七节的联系方式，申请查阅、更正或获取您在 Neo Fitness 服务器上的账号数据，开发者将在收到申请后 15 日内答复。

## 六、其他

- 本应用提供的内容不构成医疗建议，不能替代医生的诊断与治疗。
- 未满 18 周岁者不得使用本应用。
- 本政策更新时，将在本页面公布新的版本和生效日期。

## 七、联系方式

如对本政策或您的数据有任何疑问，请联系：neofitness@icloud.com

# Neo Fitness Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

Neo Fitness ("the App") is an independently developed fitness and health tracking app currently in testing. Your personal information is processed by the developer of the App (the seller listed on the App's App Store page; "the Developer"). This policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, who receives it, how long it is kept, and how you can withdraw consent and delete your data.

The App's interface is currently available in Chinese; interface labels below are given in Chinese with English translations.

## 1. Data stored on your device

Data the App reads from Apple Health, and the workouts, meals, body measurements, weight, blood pressure, lab and medical reports and other records you enter in the App, are stored only on your device by default and are used to generate health and training trends.

With your permission, the App writes the weight, waist circumference, blood pressure, nutrition intake, body composition (body fat percentage and lean body mass) and workouts (including heart rate, energy burned and workout effort scores during workouts) you record to Apple Health.

## 2. Data sent when Neo Intelligence is enabled

Neo Intelligence covers AI-generated training plans during onboarding, chat answers, automatic post-workout analysis, automatic analysis of meals and body measurements, weekly and monthly training reports, and the daily organization of chats into memories. It is enabled only after you give consent; while it is not enabled, the App sends no AI processing requests. When you sign in, manage or delete your account, the App still communicates with Apple and the Neo Fitness server.

Once enabled, to generate answers the App sends the following data directly to its large language model partners for processing. The partners' servers are located in mainland China, and your data is transferred to mainland China for processing:

- Basic body information: age, sex, height, weight, body composition;
- Heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, VO2 max;
- Sleep records;
- Workout and activity records, body measurements;
- Food and nutrition intake, meal plans and supplement settings;
- Blood pressure records (if you have recorded any);
- Lab and medical report files you choose to have the AI read;
- Photos, videos, PDFs, text files and audio files you attach in chats;
- What you enter in onboarding questions and chats, and memories organized from your chats.

When you use voice input, the recording is sent to the large language model partners and transcribed into text.

The App does not request access to reproductive health data (such as menstrual, ovulation or pregnancy records).

## 3. Recipients

| Recipient | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Neo Fitness server (located in Beijing, mainland China) | Verifies Apple sign-in, stores necessary account information and AI usage, applies individual limits to AI usage, issues temporary AI access credentials and processes deletion requests |
| Apple | Sign-in authentication and authorization revocation |
| Large language model partners (located in mainland China) | Generate answers from the submitted data; transcribe voice input |
| PubMed service operated by the U.S. National Library of Medicine (NLM) (located in the United States) | Literature search: English search terms and device IP address |

Except for PubMed, the recipients above are located in mainland China, and the related data is transferred to mainland China for processing.

The large language model partners receive data only after you enable Neo Intelligence and use it only to generate answers and transcribe voice input for you. The partners are contractually bound to protect it in a manner consistent with this policy; their data processing terms prohibit using your data to train or improve models. The partners may retain data generated by each request for a limited period under their terms and applicable law; the Developer cannot delete data retained by the partners.

When you ask Neo Intelligence a question involving scientific literature, the App sends English search terms to the PubMed service operated by the U.S. National Library of Medicine (NLM); the search terms and your device's IP address are transferred to the United States.

The Developer does not sell your data, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train any model.

## 4. Retention

- The Neo Fitness server stores account identifiers, display names, notes added by the Developer to identify accounts, individual limits, registration and last-use times, App versions, weekly AI usage statistics and request identifiers used to prevent the same request from being counted twice against the limit, for sign-in and quota management. The Developer can view account and usage lists. Account information and related management records are retained until account deletion. Weekly AI usage statistics and request identifiers are retained for 35 days and then deleted automatically, or immediately upon account deletion; temporary sign-in records are valid for 5 minutes and are removed after they expire. Apple authorization tokens are not stored long term.
- Positive and negative ratings are stored only on your device and are not uploaded to the Neo Fitness server. Chats, health data and files are not stored on the Neo Fitness server.
- Server logs do not contain questions, answers, health data, IP addresses or sign-in tokens.

## 5. Your rights, withdrawing consent and deleting data

- **Withdraw consent**: In the "Neo Intelligence" section of "设置" (Settings), tap "个性、自定义与隐私" (Personality, Customization & Privacy) — also available from "设置" (Settings) in the side menu of the chat page — and turn off the "启用" (Enable) switch. The App immediately stops sending new AI data; requests already sent when you turn it off will still complete. You can still sign in to, manage and delete your account.
- **Clear Neo Intelligence content**: In the "删除数据" (Delete Data) section of the same page, tap "清除 Neo Intelligence 内容" (Clear Neo Intelligence Content); you do not need to turn off Neo Intelligence first. This deletes all on-device chats, attachments and unlogged meal drafts in chats, as well as positive and negative ratings, memories, "我的要求" (My Requirements), "改动记录" (Change Log) and the locally cached personal profile. The account, individual limit, weekly AI usage statistics and request identifiers are retained. On-device workouts, meals, lab and medical reports, body composition records and onboarding records are unaffected. Data retained by the large language model partners is not included. This action cannot be undone.
- **Delete account**: In the "账号" (Account) section of the same page, tap your account, then tap "删除账号" (Delete Account) and confirm. The server immediately deletes your account information, individual limit, weekly AI usage statistics, request identifiers and management records; on-device Neo Intelligence content is cleared and Apple authorization is revoked at the same time. Temporary records used during deletion are removed once cleanup is complete. If the deletion request cannot be completed, you can retry. If Apple authorization cannot be completed, you can tap "无法完成 Apple 授权，继续删除" (Unable to complete Apple authorization, continue deleting) and confirm; after the account is deleted, the App prompts you to revoke Neo Fitness's authorization manually in your Apple Account settings: on your iPhone, open "Settings", tap your name, tap "Sign in with Apple", select Neo Fitness, then tap "Delete" and follow the on-screen steps to confirm. Account deletion cannot be undone. On-device workouts, meals, lab and medical reports, body composition records and onboarding records are unaffected.
- **Sign-out and device data**: Signing out clears only local sign-in credentials, temporary AI access credentials and cached usage, retaining local records. Switching Apple accounts requires confirmation to clear the previous account's on-device Neo Intelligence content. Accounts do not provide health-record cloud synchronization. Signing out or deleting an account cannot remotely erase data on other offline devices. Previously issued temporary AI access credentials may remain valid for up to 300 seconds. Deleting the App removes the remaining on-device health, body composition and onboarding records. Data already written to Apple Health must be managed or deleted in Apple Health; deleting the App does not delete it.
- **Access, correction and copies**: You may request access to, correction of, or a copy of your account data on the Neo Fitness server using the contact details in Section 7. The Developer will respond within 15 days of receiving the request.

## 6. Other

- Content provided by the App is not medical advice and does not replace diagnosis or treatment by a physician.
- The App must not be used by anyone under 18.
- When this policy changes, the new version and its effective date will be posted on this page.
