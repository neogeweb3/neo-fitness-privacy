# Neo Fitness 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 24 日

Neo Fitness（以下简称「本应用」）是一款个人开发、处于测试阶段的健身与健康记录应用。本政策说明本应用收集哪些数据、如何使用、发送给谁、保存多久，以及你如何撤回同意和删除数据。

## 一、保存在你设备上的数据

本应用从「健康」App 读取的数据，以及你在本应用中记录的训练、饮食、围度、体重、血压、化验单与检查报告等，默认只保存在你的设备上，用于生成健康与训练趋势。

## 二、启用 Neo Intelligence 后发送的数据

Neo Intelligence 包括新用户引导生成训练计划、对话问答、训练完成及饮食、围度、周期复盘的自动分析，以及每日将对话整理为记忆。只有在你同意后才会启用；未同意时，本应用不向服务器发送任何请求。

启用后，为生成回答，本应用会将以下数据直接发送至第三方 AI 服务处理，该服务的服务器位于中国内地：

- 身体基本信息：年龄、性别、身高、体重、体成分；
- 心率、静息心率、心率变异性、最大摄氧量；
- 睡眠记录；
- 训练与活动记录、身体围度；
- 饮食与营养摄入、餐单与补剂设置；
- 血压记录（如你记录过）；
- 你选择让 AI 读取的化验单与检查报告文件；
- 你在引导问题、对话中填写的内容，以及由对话整理出的记忆。

使用语音输入时，录音会发送至第三方 AI 服务转写为文字。

本应用不申请读取生殖健康类数据（如月经、排卵、妊娠相关记录）。

## 三、数据的接收方

| 接收方 | 用途 |
|---|---|
| Neo Fitness 服务器（位于中国内地） | 发放访问凭证 |
| 第三方 AI 服务（位于中国内地） | 根据提交的数据生成回答；语音转写 |

第三方 AI 服务承诺不将你的数据用于模型训练；它会依照法律法规保存调用时产生的数据，保存期限以其自身规定为准，Neo Fitness 无法删除这部分数据。

当你向 Neo Intelligence 提出涉及文献的问题时，本应用会将英文检索词发送至位于美国的美国国家医学图书馆 NCBI PubMed 服务，对方将收到检索词和设备 IP 地址，该传输涉及数据出境。

Neo Fitness 不出售你的数据，不将其用于广告，也不将其用于训练任何模型。

## 四、保存期限

- Neo Fitness 服务器不保存你的问题、回答、健康数据、文件或身份信息。
- 服务器运行日志不包含你的问题、回答、健康数据或 IP 地址。

## 五、撤回同意与删除数据

- **撤回同意**：在 App 内的「Neo Intelligence 设置」页面（可从「设置」或对话页的菜单进入）关闭 Neo Intelligence。关闭后本应用立即停止发送数据；关闭那一刻已经发出的请求会照常完成。
- **删除服务器上的数据**：关闭时选择「同时删除服务器上的数据」；也可随时在同一页面的「删除数据」一栏点按「删除所有数据」，无需先关闭 Neo Intelligence。服务器上与你相关的全部数据将被删除，且只删除你的数据；第三方 AI 服务依法保存的调用数据不在此列。删除操作无法撤销。
- **删除设备上的数据**：「删除所有数据」将删除本机的全部对话、附件、记忆、「我的要求」、「改动记录」和缓存的档案；保存在本机的健康记录（化验单与检查报告）、体成分记录和新用户引导记录不在此列，删除本应用即可一并清除。

## 六、其他

- 本应用提供的内容不构成医疗建议，不能替代医生的诊断与治疗。
- 未满 18 周岁者不得使用本应用。
- 本政策更新时，将在本页面公布新的版本和生效日期。

## 七、联系方式

如对本政策或你的数据有任何疑问，请联系：neofitness@icloud.com

# Neo Fitness Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

Neo Fitness ("the App") is an independently developed fitness and health tracking app currently in testing. This policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, who receives it, how long it is kept, and how you can withdraw consent and delete your data.

## 1. Data stored on your device

Data the App reads from Apple Health, and the workouts, meals, body measurements, weight, blood pressure, lab reports and other records you enter in the App, are stored only on your device by default and are used to generate health and training trends.

## 2. Data sent when Neo Intelligence is enabled

Neo Intelligence covers AI-generated training plans during onboarding, chat answers, automatic reviews after workouts and of meals, measurements and training periods, and the daily organization of chats into memories. It is enabled only after you give consent. Until you consent, the App sends no requests to any server.

Once enabled, to generate answers the App sends the following data directly to a third-party AI service for processing; that service's servers are located in mainland China:

- Basic body information: age, sex, height, weight, body composition;
- Heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, VO2 max;
- Sleep records;
- Workout and activity records, body measurements;
- Food and nutrition intake, meal plans and supplement settings;
- Blood pressure records (if you have recorded any);
- Lab and medical report files you choose to have the AI read;
- What you enter in onboarding questions and chats, and memories organized from your chats.

When you use voice input, the recording is sent to the third-party AI service and transcribed into text.

The App does not request access to reproductive health data (such as menstrual, ovulation or pregnancy records).

## 3. Recipients

| Recipient | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Neo Fitness server (located in mainland China) | Issues access credentials |
| Third-party AI service (located in mainland China) | Generates answers from the submitted data; transcribes voice input |

The third-party AI service commits not to use your data for model training. It retains data generated by each call as required by law, for periods governed by its own terms; Neo Fitness cannot delete that data.

When you ask Neo Intelligence a question involving scientific literature, the App sends English search terms to NCBI PubMed at the United States National Library of Medicine in the United States; the service receives the search terms and your device IP address, and this transfer involves sending data abroad.

Neo Fitness does not sell your data, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train any model.

## 4. Retention

- The Neo Fitness server does not store your questions, answers, health data, files or identity information.
- Server logs never contain your questions, answers, health data or IP address.

## 5. Withdrawing consent and deleting data

- **Withdraw consent**: In the App, open the Neo Intelligence Settings page (from Settings or the chat menu) and turn off Neo Intelligence. The App stops sending data immediately; a request already sent at that moment will still complete.
- **Delete server data**: When turning it off, choose to also delete data on the server; you can also tap "Delete All Data" in the "Delete Data" section on the same page at any time, without turning off Neo Intelligence first. All data on the server related to you will be deleted, and only your data is affected; data the third-party AI service retains as required by law is not included. Deletion cannot be undone.
- **Delete on-device data**: "Delete All Data" deletes all on-device chats, attachments, memories, "My Requirements", the "Change Log" and the cached profile. Health records stored on the device (lab and medical reports), body composition records and onboarding records are not included; deleting the App removes them as well.

## 6. Other

- Content provided by the App is not medical advice and does not replace diagnosis or treatment by a physician.
- The App must not be used by anyone under 18.
- When this policy changes, the new version and its effective date will be posted on this page.

## 7. Contact

For any questions about this policy or your data, contact: neofitness@icloud.com
